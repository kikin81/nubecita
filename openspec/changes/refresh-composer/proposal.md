## Why

Writing a post in Nubecita feels like filling in a form: a boxed field with a floating "Compose post" label, a row of option chips under it, and Post up in the top bar. Nothing shows *which account* is posting, which multi-account support will need. On short screens (the Samsung Fold 8 cover display, landscape phones) the composer also breaks: the body doesn't scroll and nothing reserves room for the keyboard, so the add-image button and anything below the field end up behind the keyboard.

A throwaway spike (2026-10-30, Fold 8 cover screen + Pixel 10 Pro Fold emulator unfolded) showed an M3 Expressive floating toolbar with Post as its FAB rides the keyboard correctly in both the phone layout and the tablet `adaptiveDialog()` card, with no dialog-specific code. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-composer-refresh-design.md`. Epic: `nubecita-5nbm`.

## What Changes

- **New shared current-account source** in `:core:profile`: a cached `StateFlow` of the signed-in account (DID, handle, display name, avatar URL), seeded from the session and refreshed once per session. `DefaultOutgoingPostStore` moves onto it; the composer reads it; a future account switcher updates it. A bench fake supplies a fixed account (making `:core:profile` a flavored module).
- **Posting identity in the composer**: the account's avatar beside the text and its display name (handle fallback) on a small line above it.
- **Borderless text field**: no outline, no floating label, placeholder only. Over-limit is signalled by the character counter alone. **BREAKING** (spec): the over-limit outline requirement goes away.
- **Scrollable body with correct IME handling**: single IME owner, bottom padding so the toolbar never covers content; @-mention suggestions brought into view when they appear.
- **Quiet summary line** under the text showing the current language and (top-level posts) audience, replacing the at-a-glance state the chips gave.
- **M3 `HorizontalFloatingToolbar`** (standard colors) with Add image, GIF, Language and Audience (Settings icon), and Post as the toolbar's FAB. Always visible; rides the keyboard. **BREAKING** (spec): Post moves out of the top app bar; the options chip row is removed.
- **Taller attachment thumbnails** in the existing chip strip (reorder, ALT badges, remove unchanged).
- Supersedes `nubecita-86m`.

## Non-goals

- Multi-account switching (only the current account is displayed).
- New authoring actions (music, stickers, polls, location).
- Threadgate/postgate changes; the audience picker is reused unchanged.
- Changing the reply parent card (stays above the text, three-line cap).
- An M3 carousel for photos (the spike tried one; the owner kept the existing strip).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `core-current-account`: a shared, cached source of the signed-in account's identity (DID, handle, display name, avatar), its refresh and sign-out lifecycle, and its bench fake.

### Modified Capabilities
- `feature-composer`: the language chip becomes a toolbar action plus a summary-line entry; the Post button becomes the floating toolbar's FAB and the over-limit outline is removed; the top bar holds close + counter (drafts reservation kept); suggestions are brought into view in a scrolling body; new requirements for the identity row, borderless field, scrolling/IME behaviour, floating toolbar and summary line.

## Impact

- **Code**: `:core:profile` (new repository, Hilt binding, `src/bench/` fake, `nubecita.android.flavors`), `:core:posts` (`DefaultOutgoingPostStore` refactor), `:feature:composer:impl` (`ComposerScreen`, `ComposerState`, `ComposerViewModel`, removal of `ComposerOptionsChipRow` and the chip composables' chip role, attachment thumbnail sizing).
- **Dependencies**: `:feature:composer:impl` gains `:core:profile`. No new libraries; `HorizontalFloatingToolbar` is in the current M3 `1.5.0-beta02` (opt-in `@ExperimentalMaterial3ExpressiveApi`).
- **Baseline deviations**: none from MVI / Compose / Hilt / Room / Coil. The composer keeps its sanctioned `TextFieldState` editor exception (now via `BasicTextField(state = …)`).
- **Build**: `:core:profile` becomes flavored, so its task names change to `…ProductionDebug…`.
- **Tests**: composer screenshot baselines change (regenerated by the CI bot); new unit tests for the account source and composer identity state.
